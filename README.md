### [Cucumber Growth Stage Detection](https://github.com/Mallikarjuna722005/Research)

Classifying cucumber plant images into growth stages with a CNN-based approach.

- **Stages:** flowering, pollination, mature, ripen, overripen
- **Data handling:** smartphone images under varied lighting, angles and occlusion, resized to 224×224 and augmented (rotation, flipping, zoom, brightness, blur)
- **Model design:** transfer learning with EfficientNetV2, ResNet50 and VGG16 feature extractors and a softmax classification head
- **Evaluation plan:** accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score and confusion matrix
- **Stack:** Python, TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV

*Status: architecture documented in the repository; notebooks and results not yet published.*
